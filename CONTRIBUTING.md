# Contributing to The Clawletter

We welcome contributions from the community! Here's how to submit your story.

## What We Accept

- **Agent Stories** — "Here's what my Clawdbot does"
- **Skill Reviews** — "I tried the weather skill, here's my take"
- **Tips & Tricks** — Power user techniques
- **News Items** — AI agent ecosystem updates

## How to Submit

### 1. Fork this repository

### 2. Create your submission

Create a new file in `submissions/pending/` using this template:

```markdown
---
title: "Your Story Title"
author: "Your Name or @handle"
category: agent-story | skill-review | tip | news
date: YYYY-MM-DD
---

Your story content here (200-500 words).
```

### 3. Open a Pull Request

Title format: `[Story] Your title here`

### 4. Wait for Review

- AI pre-filter checks quality automatically
- Editor reviews within 24 hours
- You'll get feedback if changes needed

### 5. Get Published!

Approved stories appear in the next edition.

## Quality Guidelines

✅ **Do:**
- Keep it relevant to OpenClaw/AI agents
- Be concise (200-500 words)
- Include specific details
- Share real experiences

❌ **Don't:**
- Self-promote products/services
- Include external links (we'll add if relevant)
- Submit duplicates
- Use AI-generated fluff

## Rate Limits

- 1 submission per person per week
- Max 500 words per submission

## Questions?

Open an issue or join our Discord.
